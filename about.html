The Rooted Robin story

Beauty with deeper roots

The Rooted Robin grew from something deeply personal.

I'm a woman, a mother of two, a maker, and someone who has walked through seasons that completely changed the way I look at health, home, and the things we surround ourselves with. My own journey through mental health struggles and cancer made me more intentional, not only about caring for myself, but also about the kind of life I wanted to create for my children.

I began looking more closely at the everyday things we put on our bodies, bring into our homes, and use to care for ourselves. I wanted simplicity. I wanted beautiful things that felt special without asking me to compromise what mattered most to me: our health.

And somewhere along the way, The Rooted Robin began to take root.

Inspired by the forests, flowers, herbs, and quiet wildness of Wisconsin's Northwoods, I create handcrafted goods slowly, thoughtfully, and with intention. Some are made to bring a little comfort to an ordinary day. Others are created to carry meaning, tell a story, or simply remind us that there is still beauty to be found in the smallest things.

This little business is an extension of the life I'm trying to build, one that is slower, more connected, more intentional, and a little closer to nature.

The Rooted Robin is still growing, just as I am. Every batch made, every piece created, and every person who chooses to support my work becomes a small part of this story.

From my hands and my little corner of Wisconsin to yours-thank you for being here.
